# Recommended Monetization Structure

## 1. Free Tier (critical)

You almost certainly want a generous free tier.

Example:
- Access to a few games/tools
- Limited saves/progress
- Basic teacher account
- Public templates

This is especially important in education because:
- schools move slowly
- teachers want to experiment first
- students create viral adoption internally

The free tier becomes your acquisition engine.

# 2. Individual Premium Subscription

This is probably your core revenue stream early on.

Suggested pricing:
- €4–8/month individual
- OR €40–70/year

Include:
- all apps/tools
- cloud sync
- unlimited projects/classes
- advanced calculators/games
- export/import
- progress tracking
- AI-assisted learning features later

# 3. Education / Classroom License (MOST IMPORTANT)

Schools and tutoring organizations do not want:
- per-game purchases
- app-store style monetization
- confusing upgrade paths

They want:
- predictable billing
- seat management
- admin controls
- compliance
- onboarding support

Suggested pricing structures:
- Small classrooms: €10–25/month per teacher
- Schools: €300–2000/year depending on scale
- District/organization: custom pricing

# 4. Coupon Strategy (your 20% idea)

Instead of:
“20% off signup”

Better framing:
- “Partner educator discount”
- “Founding classroom access”
- “Early adopter educator pricing”
- “Education network pricing”

Educational buyers psychologically prefer:
- institutional partnership
- educator support
- grant/sponsorship framing

# 5. Avoid One-Time Pricing as Primary

One-time pricing creates problems:
- ongoing hosting costs
- continuous support burden
- no recurring revenue
- hard to fund new app development

You can still offer:
- lifetime licenses
- self-hosted editions
- offline packs

…but treat those as niche offerings.

# Recommended Structure

Tier | Price | Purpose
Free | €0 | acquisition
Premium Individual | €5/mo | parents/power users
Teacher Pro | €15/mo | educators
School License | €500+/yr | sustainable business
Enterprise/White Label | custom | large orgs

# Most Important Recommendation

Do NOT optimize for maximum revenue immediately.

Optimize for:
1. adoption
2. educator trust
3. retention
4. recurring usage
5. institutional embedding

Once teachers build lesson plans around your ecosystem, monetization becomes much easier.
